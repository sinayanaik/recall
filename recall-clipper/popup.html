What to save
Pages

Pages are cut before headings and between paragraphs, never through a picture or code, and each page is trimmed to its content. On the page, drag a break to move it, click to add one, ✕ to remove one.

One page as tall as the content (a page over 200 in is split into a few). Very long pages are slower to scroll in any PDF reader — Smart pages read better.

Pictures

High is 200 DPI at JPEG 85%, Medium 150 DPI at 75%, Low 100 DPI at 60%. Pictures, from any site, are resized to the size they print at.

Original keeps each picture's size; WebP and AVIF pictures still become JPEG, which PDF readers draw far faster.

Extras

Tokens: {title} {url} {date} {page} {pages}. A header or footer needs at least 0.45 in of margin, which is added for you. With a footer, smart pages all keep the same height.

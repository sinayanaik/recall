Recall Drag to Canvas

  1. Hold Alt and point at any part of the page. It is outlined, with a ⠿ grip above it.
  2. Alt+↑ / ↓ (or Alt and the wheel) takes in more of the page, or less.
  3. Drag the ⠿ onto a page of a file’s Canvas in Recall. It lands as Blocks. Hold Shift as you let go for a picture of it, exactly as it looks.

This page can’t be used: the browser keeps its own pages and the extension store to itself.

Nothing is uploaded: what you drag goes straight into Recall, and pictures are stored there with your other pictures.
